My Reflection

My Growth in Web Design

There are many goals that I strive to achieve as a coach, but one of them is supporting the success of all my students. I want to guide each athlete toward their goals and help them become the best skater they can be. My goal is to make a positive impact on every student, not only as their coach but also as someone who supports and encourages them. I want my students to feel confident in themselves and know that I will help them reach their goals.

I have been coaching for eight years and have experience working with skaters of all different ages and levels. I have worked at Jefferson City Ice Arena as a choreographer for their ice shows and as a Learn to Skate instructor. These experiences have helped me grow as a coach and taught me how important it is to make skating an enjoyable experience for every skater.

In the St. Louis area, I have taught group Learn to Skate classes at both the Kirkwood Ice Arena and the Webster Ice Arena. I will also be involved in the Nutcracker on Ice production. Through private lessons, I have worked with competitive figure skaters ranging from beginners to the collegiate level. I have also coached hockey players, from beginner-level players working toward joining a team to players improving their position.

Moving forward, I want to continue growing as a coach and helping each of my students reach their fullest potential. Every skater has their own definition of success, and I know that I can help them get there. Whether a student wants to learn the basics or be competitive, I want to support them throughout their journey. Seeing my students make progress and accomplish their goals is the most fulfilling part of being a coach.

Olivia Eaton | October 18, 2026
