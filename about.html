Meet the Coach

As a skater myself, my love for the sport is what drives my passion for coaching. Coaching allows me to motivate athletes and build their confidence to help them grow both on and off the ice while working towards their goals. I work with skaters of all ages and levels and focus on making lessons both productive and encouraging.

Services

Liv to Skate offers private figure skating lessons, hockey skills coaching, and off-ice training.
