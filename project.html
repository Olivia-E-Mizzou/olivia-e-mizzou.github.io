Skating Services

Liv to Skate offers personalized coaching to help figure skaters and hockey players improve their skills, confidence, and performance on and off the ice.

Private Figure-Skating Lessons

Individual lessons are designed around each skater’s experience, goals, and areas for improvement.

Sample Lesson Focus

  • Edges and turns
  • Jumps and spins
  • Balance and body position
  • Confidence and performance skills

Hockey-Skills Coaching

Hockey-skills coaching helps players become stronger, faster, and more controlled skaters.

Sample Training Focus

  • Forward and backward skating
  • Speed and acceleration
  • Stops and direction changes
  • Edge control and agility

Off-Ice Training

Off-ice exercises support the strength, flexibility, balance, and coordination needed for skating.

Sample Exercise Focus

  • Core strength
  • Balance and stability
  • Jump technique
  • Flexibility and mobility

Choreography

Choreography sessions help skaters create programs that connect their technical skills with music and personal expression.

Program Development

  • Music interpretation
  • Transitions and footwork
  • Program structure
  • Presentation and expression
